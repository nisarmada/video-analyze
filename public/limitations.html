Limitations

RepTrack counts push-ups, squats, and pull-ups from a camera or a video file. It is a workout aid. It is not a validated fitness test, and it is not medical or coaching advice.

What stays on this device

  • Camera frames and uploaded clips are processed in the browser. They are not uploaded.
  • The pose model and its runtime are part of this app. Counting does not call a vision API.
  • Workout history, range-of-motion settings, and an optional person outline are stored in this browser’s local storage.
  • JSON and CSV export download a file only when you ask for one.

What “Select person” does

It stores a coarse outline of the head and shoulders from the pose model so that, when more than one person is in frame, the counter can prefer the one you selected. It is not face recognition. There is no identity database. If nobody in frame is close to that outline, the counter follows the largest person instead. Turning your head, or a very different distance from the camera combined with occlusion, can miss the match. Clear the selection at any time.

How a set is recorded

  • Reps are added only after you start a workout.
  • The rest timer closes a set after that many seconds without a new rep. Time keeps passing if you step out of frame while the camera is still running.
  • Stop video pauses the camera. The open set stays on screen. It is saved when you press Finish, or it closes on its own if you start the camera again after the rest time has already passed.
  • An uploaded clip plays once. Replaying it inside the player would count those reps again.

When the count will be wrong

  • The joint angle is measured on the 2D image, so a steep camera angle changes it.
  • A hidden elbow, knee, or hip drops the frame. Loose clothing and poor light do the same.
  • Auto detect is a heuristic. Lock the movement when the guess is unsure.
  • Very fast or partial reps can be missed. Extra movement can be counted.
  • The thresholds are the ones in Range of motion. They are not calibrated to a published test.
  • There is no labelled accuracy study in this project. Do not quote an error rate for it.
