Privacy

RepTrack runs pose estimation in your browser. Camera frames and uploaded clips stay on your device. The pose runtime is served with this app, so counting does not send frames to a vision service.

  • No account is required.
  • Workout history, range-of-motion settings, and an optional person outline are stored in this browser’s local storage.
  • The person outline is a few pose points used to prefer one person in a crowded frame. It is not a face template. You can clear it in the app.
  • You can clear saved workouts from the app. Export downloads a file only if you choose it.
